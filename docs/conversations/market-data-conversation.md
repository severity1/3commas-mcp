# Market Data Conversation Examples

Example user prompts for market data tools:

## Trading Pairs Research
- "What BTC trading pairs are available on Binance?"
- "Show me all available trading pairs across all supported exchanges"
- "Which altcoin pairs have the highest volume on OKX?"
- "What trading pairs are available for ETH on Binance?"

## Rate and Limit Information
- "What's the current BTC rate and what are the trading limits?"
- "Get current rates and trading limits for ETH/USDT on Binance"
- "I want to place a $1,000 order. What are the minimum and maximum limits?"
- "Show me current market rates for all major cryptocurrencies"

## Exchange Capabilities
- "Which exchanges support DCA bots and what features are available?"
- "Show me all supported markets and their trading capabilities"
- "What exchanges can I use for automated trading?"
- "Which exchange has the most features for bot trading?"

## Bot Configuration Research
- "I want to create a DCA bot for altcoins. Help me research the best setup"
- "Which exchanges and pairs would work best for a $50,000 DCA bot?"
- "Validate if my proposed bot configuration fits within exchange limits"
- "I want to create a $50,000 DCA bot with $1,000 base orders. Is this possible?"

## Market Analysis
- "Are there arbitrage opportunities between Binance and OKX?"
- "Help me find the most liquid trading pairs for my strategy"
- "What are the best pairs for diversified portfolio allocation?"
- "Compare trading fees and spreads across different exchanges"
