# Account Management Conversation Examples

Example user prompts for account management tools:

## Account Information
- "Show me all my connected exchanges and their status"
- "Get my account summary with balance and profit information"
- "Tell me about my account performance and settings for ID 12345678"
- "How is my overall portfolio performing across all exchanges?"

## Exchange Validation
- "I want to create a DCA bot for BTC/USDT. Which exchanges can I use?"
- "Why can't I create bots? Are my exchange connections working?"
- "Which exchange should I use for automated trading?"

## Balance History
- "Show me how my account balance has changed over the last 30 days"
- "How did my portfolio perform during the August market volatility?"
- "Show me how my account balance has changed this month for ID 12345678"
- "How has my portfolio performed over time?"
- "Show me my performance during January 2025"
- "How well are my DCA bots performing compared to manual trading?"
