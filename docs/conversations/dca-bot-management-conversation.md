# DCA Bot Management Conversation Examples

Example user prompts for DCA bot management tools:

## Bot Details
- "Can you show me the details of my DCA bot with ID 12345678? I want to see its current configuration and any active deals."
- "My bot 12345678 hasn't made any trades recently. Can you check what's going on?"
- "Show me the performance of bot 12345678. I want to understand if my current settings are optimal."

## Bot Portfolio
- "Can you show me all my DCA bots? I want to see which ones are active and get an overview of my portfolio."
- "Show me only my long position bots that are trading on account 12345678. I want to see how my long strategy is performing."
- "List my DCA bots filtered by strategy and sort by profit"

## Strategy Information
- "What trading strategies are available for DCA bots? I'm thinking about creating a new bot and want to understand my options."
- "Show me all available DCA bot strategies and help me choose the best one"

## Performance Analysis
- "Can you show me the profit history for my bot 12345678 over the last 30 days? I want to see the daily performance trends."
- "Get profit data for bot 12345678 over the last 90 days"
- "Show me the daily profit analytics for bot 12345678"

## Trading Restrictions
- "Before I create a new DCA bot, I want to see which trading pairs are blacklisted. Can you show me the current restrictions?"
- "What trading pairs are currently restricted for DCA bots?"
- "Show me the blacklisted pairs and safe alternatives for bot creation"
